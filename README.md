# FUTURE_DS_03
# 📊 Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes marketing campaign performance using the **Bank Marketing Dataset**. The objective is to evaluate customer conversion behavior, identify drop-off points in the marketing funnel, and provide actionable recommendations to improve conversion rates.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer conversion performance
- Measure marketing conversion rate
- Identify campaign drop-off patterns
- Evaluate contact methods and customer segments
- Generate actionable business recommendations

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Kaggle Bank Marketing Dataset

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle – Bank Marketing Dataset

The dataset includes customer demographics, campaign details, contact methods, previous campaign outcomes, and whether a customer subscribed to a term deposit.

---

## 📊 Dashboard KPIs

- Total Leads
- Total Conversions
- Total Non-Conversions
- Conversion Rate
- Average Campaign Contacts
- Average Balance

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

- Conversions by Contact Method
- Conversions by Previous Campaign Outcome
- Monthly Conversion Analysis
- Conversions by Occupation
- Customer Distribution by Education

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Overall conversion rate is **47.38%**.
- Cellular contact generated more successful conversions than telephone.
- Previous campaign success positively influenced future conversions.
- Conversion performance varied across occupations and months.
- Customers with secondary education formed the largest customer segment.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Prioritize high-performing contact methods.
- Target customer segments with stronger conversion rates.
- Improve follow-up strategies for unsuccessful previous campaigns.
- Optimize campaigns during high-performing months.
- Use customer segmentation to personalize marketing efforts.
